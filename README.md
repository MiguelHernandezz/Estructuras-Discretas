## 📘 What the subject covers

> **Estructuras Discretas (780008)** · 6 ECTS · 1st year · 2nd semester · contents from the 2024-25 teaching guide · [official subject page](https://www.uah.es/es/estudios/estudios-oficiales/grados/asignatura/Estructuras-Discretas-780008/)

| Unit | Hours | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Logic | 8 h | Propositions and connectives, implication, equivalence, tautologies · Predicates and quantifiers · Proof methods |
| Combinatorics | 22 h | Counting principles: variations, permutations, combinations · Inclusion-exclusion · Generating functions and integer partitions · Solving linear recurrence relations |
| Graph theory | 20 h | Terminology, representation and isomorphism · Dijkstra's shortest paths, trees and spanning trees · Planar graphs and colouring · Bipartite matching · Transport networks and maximum flow |
| Discrete probability | 6 h | Discrete random variables and distributions · Monte Carlo algorithms |
